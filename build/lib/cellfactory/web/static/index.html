Growth and yield

How fast the cell grows and how much product it makes per mole of substrate, plus the growth vs. production trade-off.

Gene knockouts

Search for deletions that force the cell to make more product. FBA finds growth-coupled designs; MOMA suits products the cell has no reason to make.

Nearest native precursors

Know the compound but not the pathway? Search backwards from it to what the host already makes, and list the enzymes to clone in.

What the cell does to the product

Which host reactions consume it, which deletions protect it, and whether its charge means it needs a transporter.

Model sanity checks

Before trusting a striking result: does the model make energy from nothing, are reactions mass-balanced, which genes are essential?
